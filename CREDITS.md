Self-hosted WOFF2 subset of **Outfit** (The Outfit Project Authors), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license text is in `static/fonts/outfit-LICENSE.txt`. Both `--serif` and `--sans` point at Outfit; the site does not load or synthesize italics (`em`, `i`, and `cite` are forced upright).
