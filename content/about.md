I’m a fifth-year PhD candidate in the [Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics](https://astrophysics.uchicago.edu/) at the University of Chicago. My research explores active galactic nuclei (AGN) feedback at all scales, from outflows close to the black hole to feedback's impact on the gas in galaxy clusters. I use X-ray observations from **XRISM, Chandra, eROSITA, XMM-Newton, NuSTAR and Swift**.

Before I came to Chicago, I earned my undergraduate degree in physics at Washington University in St. Louis, where I studied the X-ray binary GX 301-2 with NICER. I then spent two years in Germany completing my master’s in astrophysics at the [Argelander-Institut für Astronomie](https://astro.uni-bonn.de/en) at Universität Bonn, studying the outskirts of the Virgo Cluster with eROSITA.
