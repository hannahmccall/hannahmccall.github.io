I’m a PhD candidate in the [Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics](https://astrophysics.uchicago.edu/) at the University of Chicago. My research explores active galactic nuclei (AGN) feedback and galaxy clusters using X-ray observations from **XRISM, Chandra, eROSITA, and XMM-Newton**.

Before Chicago, I earned my undergraduate degree in physics at Washington University in St. Louis, where I studied the X-ray binary GX 301-2 with NICER. I then spent two years in Germany completing my master’s in astrophysics at the [Argelander-Institut für Astronomie](https://astro.uni-bonn.de/en) at Universität Bonn, studying the outskirts of the Virgo Cluster with eROSITA.
